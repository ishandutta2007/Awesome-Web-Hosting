## Top Wholesale Sales & Operations Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on B2B Order Management, Sales Rep Enablement & Wholesale Commerce*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Wholesale Sales & Operations**. These tools help brands, distributors, and sales agencies manage the unique workflows of wholesale commerce — order capture, customer-specific pricing, B2B storefronts, trade show selling, and ERP integration — replacing spreadsheets, email chains, and manual order entry.

**Examples** include WizCommerce, Handshake, Pepperi, Brandwise, RepZio, NuORDER, JOOR, Elastic Suite, MarketTime, and Faire (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom B2B workflows, and transparent order data — ideal for wholesale brands prioritizing data sovereignty, developers building commerce platforms, and agencies avoiding per-user pricing.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[WizCommerce](https://wizcommerce.com/)**  
  AI-powered wholesale platform connecting sales, operations, and buying workflows across four core products: WizOrder (AI-first order-taking app for sales reps), WizShop (wholesale-native B2B ecommerce storefront), WizPay (embedded payments), and WizStudio (AI-generated catalog imagery). AI Co-Workers automate order entry from email/PDF/scan and provide sales copilot assistance. Integrates with QuickBooks, Business Central, NetSuite, Sage, and Microsoft Dynamics [citation:1][citation:14].

- **[Handshake](https://www.handshake.com/)**  
  B2B sales and catalog app for iPad enabling wholesale brands and sales agencies to write orders at trade shows, showrooms, and on the road. Exports CSV for back-office systems via API. The company has raised $25M in total funding, with Shopify among its investors [citation:15]. *Note: A separate UK company (Handshake Solutions Ltd) offers an AI-powered retail agreement platform, founded 2024 and backed by $3.2M [citation:2].*

- **[Pepperi](https://www.pepperi.com/)**  
  Comprehensive B2B platform for consumer goods manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers. Offers adaptable B2B eCommerce storefront, Sales Force Automation, Retail Execution, Direct Store Delivery (DSD), and Trade Promotions. Integrates with ERP systems and provides offline order placement, store auditing, and merchandising across iOS and Android [citation:3].

- **[Brandwise](https://www.brandwise.com/)**  
  Wholesale sales platform supporting electronic order submission, online and mobile catalogs, and sales reporting. Enables sales reps and agencies to create orders via scanner, computer, tablet, or iPad with web displays showcasing products. Managers get unified sales data from internal teams and rep agencies [citation:4].

- **[RepZio](https://www.repzio.com/)**  
  B2B sales platform with iOS App for iPad, Web App backup, B2B Direct websites, and ShopZio marketplace. Features order submission, customer management, catalog browsing, tear sheets, and extensive global settings for order routing, pricing, and multi-line manufacturer support [citation:5][citation:17].

- **[NuORDER by Lightspeed](https://www.nuorder.com/)**  
  B2B eCommerce platform serving department stores including Bloomingdale's, Nordstrom, Saks, and David Jones. Offers hundreds of automated integrations, digital linesheets, inventory visibility, replenishments, and a Marketplace reaching 500K+ retailers [citation:6][citation:18].

- **[JOOR](https://joor.com/)**  
  Leading B2B wholesale fashion platform trusted by 14,000+ brands and 700,000+ retail buyers. Handles $120B+ in wholesale transactions. Features virtual showrooms, digital linesheets, order management, JOOR Pay, and 100+ ERP integrations including Shopify, NetSuite, and SAP. Reported 60% faster order processing and 80% operational cost savings [citation:7][citation:19].

- **[Elastic Suite](https://www.elasticsuite.com/)**  
  B2B ecommerce platform for wholesale brands, with a dedicated Shopify Connector synchronizing products, customers, inventory, pricing, and orders between Shopify and Elastic. Supports customer-specific pricing, digital merchandising, and fixed-cost implementation. A client grew wholesale GMV by 439% in six months [citation:8].

- **[MarketTime](https://www.markettime.com/)**  
  B2B omnichannel commerce platform uniting retailers, brands, and sales teams. Acquired Brandwise, combining order writing, commission reconciliation, territory management, and B2B ecommerce. Release notes show active development in 2026 including promotions, order duplication, and social media links for B2B websites [citation:9][citation:16].

- **[Faire](https://www.faire.com/)**  
  Wholesale marketplace with 100,000+ brands and 4.5M unique SKUs. For large retailers, offers centralized onboarding, access to emerging brands, ACH payment, PO support, and multi-location ordering with centralized visibility. Acts as a single partner for insurance documentation and payments across participating brands [citation:10].

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Spree Commerce](https://github.com/spree/spree)**  
  Leading open-source ecommerce platform with **native B2B and wholesale capabilities**. Spree 5.6 introduces a spreadsheet-style grid ordering interface, autocomplete product search, wholesale threshold pricing, buyer organizations, spending limits, role-based purchasing (Enterprise Edition), pre-orders with ship dates and caps, and admin localization in 50+ languages with RTL support. Drops Redis requirement with a 40% smaller Docker image. BSD-3-Clause licensed [citation:11].

- **[Aiku](https://github.com/Inikoo-Ltd/aiku)**  
  Open-source white-label ERP web application with comprehensive wholesale sales and operations support. Features order capture flowing directly into warehouse, full picking/packing/label printing, carrier APIs (DPD, GLS, APC, CTT, Packeta, ITD), 3PL fulfillment with customer portal, dropshipping and marketplace sync, **Faire wholesale marketplace integration**, built-in CMS and storefront engine, CRM and marketing, accounting and payments, and a first-party MCP server with permission-scoped tools. PHP 8.4/Laravel 13/PostgreSQL 18 stack. AGPL-3.0 licensed [citation:12].

- **[Corteza](https://github.com/cortezaproject/corteza)**  
  Open-source low-code platform with CRM and workflow automation. Listed among CRM frameworks for building custom B2B sales workflows and wholesale order management. Apache 2.0 licensed [citation:13].

- **[Krayin CRM](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  
  Open-source Laravel CRM with low-code enterprise-grade features. Suitable for managing wholesale customer relationships, sales pipelines, and order tracking. MIT licensed [citation:13].

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  
  Award-winning enterprise-class open-source CRM. Extensible for wholesale account management, distributor relationships, and B2B sales tracking. AGPL-3.0 licensed [citation:13].

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  
  Open-source CRM with SPA frontend and REST API. Customizable entities for wholesale customers, order history, and territory management. GPL-3.0 licensed [citation:13].

- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  
  Modern open-source CRM with flexibility. Growing ecosystem suitable for B2B sales and wholesale customer management. AGPL-3.0 licensed [citation:13].

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  All-in-one open-source ERP with robust CRM, sales order management, inventory, and purchasing modules. Comprehensive foundation for wholesale operations. GPL-3.0 licensed [citation:13].

- **[Warpdrive](https://github.com/warpdrivedev/warpdrive)**  
  Self-hosted business-development CRM reimplementing Pipedrive's pipeline, deal workspace, and two-way Gmail sync. Next.js/TypeScript stack. Suitable for wholesale sales pipeline management [citation:13].

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)** — Modular open-source ERP and CRM for SMEs with invoicing, proposals, orders, stock, and supplier management. GPL-3.0 licensed [citation:13].
- **[YetiForce](https://github.com/YetiForceCompany/YetiForceCRM)** — Feature-rich open-source CRM with 100+ built-in modules adaptable for wholesale workflows. PHP-based [citation:13].
- **[OroCRM](https://github.com/oroinc/crm-application)** — Flexible open-source CRM for marketing, sales, and customer service. PHP/Symfony stack [citation:13].
- **[Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm)** — Fully featured modern CRM built on Frappe framework. Python-based [citation:13].
- **[DaybydayCRM](https://github.com/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM)** — Track daily workflow with leads, tasks, and clients. Lightweight PHP/Laravel stack [citation:13].
- **[Django-CRM](https://github.com/Django-CRM/Django-CRM)** — CRM for startups with sales pipeline management. Python/Django stack [citation:13].
- **[Relaticle](https://github.com/relaticle/relaticle)** — Next-gen CRM with Laravel and Filament. PHP/Laravel stack [citation:13].
- **[NextCRM](https://github.com/pdovhomilja/nextcrm-app)** — CRM with projects, invoicing, documents, and AI features. Next.js stack [citation:13].

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Spree Commerce** for B2B storefront and wholesale pricing, **Aiku** for ERP-integrated wholesale operations with Faire marketplace connectivity, **Corteza** or **Krayin** for custom CRM workflows, and **ERPNext** for full ERP with sales and inventory to build a complete self-hosted wholesale sales and operations stack.
